Live Music in Sapanca: Evenings With Music at Mare Gastro

2026-11-076 min readMare Gastro

Short answer: for dinner with live music in Sapanca, Mare Gastro's weekend evenings come with live music, and guest artists take the stage on special nights. In summer 2026, Eda Acar and her orchestra played in the open-air garden on Saturday nights. The current programme is on our Instagram account and in the booking calendar.

Where Is There Live Music in Sapanca?

Venues with live music in Sapanca are mostly around the lake and in Kırkpınar. Mare Gastro brings a fine dining kitchen and live music together in the same evening in the garden of Didi Otel: food, cocktails and the stage in one garden.

The restaurant is open Tuesday to Sunday, 09:00–00:00; live-music evenings are usually at weekends.

Where Can I Find the Current Live Music Programme?

Artists and special nights are announced on our Instagram account. In the booking calendar on our website, days with special events are marked in colour; tap a day to see the details of the event.

Is It Hard to Get a Table on a Live Music Night?

The number of tables can be limited on special nights. That's why we recommend booking in advance for a live-music evening; choosing the date, time and party size and confirming on WhatsApp takes only a few minutes.

What If I Want a Quieter Table?

Just mention it when you book; our team will try to arrange a quieter corner away from the stage. For an evening for two, also see our romantic dinner guide.

What Happens Besides Music?

Mare Gastro also hosts events other than music from time to time. For example, in September 2026 there was a workshop on wild mushrooms with mycologist Jilber Barutçiyan (@mushroomsofturkey). Upcoming events appear in the calendar in the booking section.
