A Graduation Dinner in Sapanca: Lunch with Family, Evening with Friends

November 16, 20267 min readMare Gastro

Short answer: A graduation dinner is planned in one of two ways: a lunch with the family, or a longer evening with adult friends. In Sapanca, Mare Gastro suits both, with a single rule: guests with children are welcome until 15:00, and evenings are reserved for adults. The restaurant is open 09:00–00:00 (kitchen's last order 23:00); cake and table decoration are arranged through the reservation, and organization details are planned by talking to the restaurant.

A Graduation Dinner with Family or with Friends?

A graduation table calls for an entirely different plan depending on who you are celebrating with. A family dinner spans a wide range of ages, from grandmother to little brother; at a dinner with friends the evening stretches out, and music and drinks come to the fore. If there are children at the table, plan the meal for lunchtime at Mare Gastro so that it ends before 15:00.

How Do You Build a Menu for Mixed Age Groups?

At family graduation dinners, different palates meet at one table. Mare Gastro's menu is broad enough to cover that variety (10 November 2026): 13 starters, 3 salads, 8 warm small plates, 11 main courses and 5 desserts.

  • Children (only until 15:00): Grilled Meatballs (children's menu), hamburger, fries
  • Grandparents: Oven-Baked Sea Bass in Cream Sauce, Shank Dream, artichoke in olive oil
  • Vegetarians: Hummus, Beetroot Borani, fried eggplant and peppers, salads
  • Meat lovers: Beef Tenderloin in Pepper Cream Sauce, Lamb Chops
  • Dessert for everyone: A Walk in the Forest, Mare Şekerpare, fruit plate

Current dishes and prices are on the digital menu.

A Graduation Dinner with a Class or School Group

Sometimes a graduation dinner is the plan not of one family but of a whole class. For large groups of students, three things make the evening easier: giving the final number of guests a few days ahead, deciding from the start how the bill will be split, and building the menu around shared starters.

For high school graduations with students under 18, discuss the age and time conditions by contacting the restaurant before booking; the sale of alcohol to anyone under 18 is prohibited in Turkey. The menu has non-alcoholic cocktails, milkshakes, frozen drinks and cold drinks.

Cake, Decoration and Surprises

The graduation cake and table decoration are planned when you reserve; cake is a separate item in the Extras section of the digital menu. All you need to do is state in the reservation note the message to be written on the cake, any wish for decoration in your school or department colors, and whether it is a surprise.

What Is the Best Time for Graduation Photos?

The graduation season usually falls in June and July; in those months the sun sets in Sapanca at about 20:15–20:35. For a family photo in the garden, the hour before sunset gives the softest light. Moving on to dinner afterwards leaves both the photo and the table relaxed.

Reservations and Group Planning

  1. Enter the number of guests and the date in the reservation form; in graduation season write one to two weeks ahead for a weekend.
  2. In the note, mention the graduation, your cake and decoration wishes and any allergy information; discuss organization details by contacting the restaurant. If there are children at the table, plan the meal before 15:00.
  3. For large families, consider cutting down the number of cars; parking is free and there is valet.
  4. For family members coming from far away, consider staying at Didi Otel in the same garden.

A Gift for the Graduate: A Dinner

Giving an experience instead of an object as a graduation gift is increasingly common. A dinner right next to the lake can be a gift the graduate shares with loved ones and remembers for years. You can make the reservation yourself and mention in the note that it is a gift, and have the cake and table prepared as a surprise.

For budget planning, see our post on celebration prices and menu options; we do not give price figures, because current prices are always on the digital menu.

Raising Glasses and a Short Speech

The most remembered moment of a graduation dinner is usually a short speech by a family elder or a close friend. Putting the speech right before the cake catches the moment when the table's attention is gathered. A speech of no more than two or three minutes that tells a single memory from the graduate's journey works far better than a long list.

4 Suggestions for Graduation Photos

  1. Take the photos before the meal, in the garden an hour before sunset.
  2. Bring the cap and gown; the green backdrop of the garden gives a nice contrast.
  3. Record the cake moment as a video too; a short clip becomes the most-watched recording years later.
  4. Take the table photo before the plates arrive, with the decoration and the flowers.

For Family Members Coming from Afar

If there are family members coming from another city for the graduation ceremony, planning the journey and accommodation together with the dinner makes the evening easier. Mare Gastro is about 1.5 hours from Istanbul by car; parking is free and there is valet. For directions, see our post on how to get to Mare Gastro, and for those who want to stay the night, consider Didi Otel in the same garden.

Garden for a Summer Graduation, Dining Room in Winter

Most university graduations are in June–July, while some programs hold theirs at the end of the term, in the winter months. In summer the evening is warm and long, so a garden table comes to the fore; for winter graduations the glass-walled indoor dining room is both warm and bright enough for photos. State your space preference in the reservation; if the weather changes, the table can be moved inside.

Choosing the Menu with the Elders in Mind

At family graduation dinners, grandparents are usually at the table too. Lighter, familiar plates make the table easier for them: artichoke in olive oil, Oven-Baked Sea Bass in Cream Sauce, slow-cooked Shank Dream or Neighbour's Salad. Ordering the spicy or raw fish plates (Spicy Atom, Bonito Carpaccio) for the middle of the table and leaving them for those who want to try lets everyone eat at their own pace.
