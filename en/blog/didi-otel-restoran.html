The Didi Otel Restaurant: Fine Dining in the Garden, Mare Gastro

Last updated: November 16, 20266 min readMare Gastro

Short answer: Didi Otel's restaurant is Mare Gastro; it is in the hotel's garden in Sapanca, right next to the lake. The restaurant is open to everyone, not only hotel guests: breakfast, lunch and dinner 09:00–00:00 Tuesday to Sunday, with Chef Doğan Anapa's à la carte Aegean-Mediterranean menu.

What Is the Relationship Between Didi Otel and Mare Gastro?

Mare Gastro is the restaurant opened within Didi Otel and serving in the hotel's garden. Its concept was designed by Kadir Çiçek within Didi Otel, and its kitchen is run by Executive Chef Doğan Anapa. The hotel and the restaurant share the same entrance, the same parking lot and the same garden; but Mare Gastro has its own menu, its own reservation system and its own team.

For the hotel's rooms and accommodation information, see sapancadidiotel.com; for the restaurant's menu and reservations, this site.

In short, Mare Gastro serves not only guests, like a "hotel restaurant", but everyone who comes to Sapanca for dinner.

An Evening in the Garden: Location and Atmosphere

The garden, surrounded by palms and pines, consists of outdoor tables around the pool and a glass-walled indoor dining room. The garden is preferred in summer and the dining room on cool evenings; both are served from the same kitchen.

What Is on the Menu?

As of 10 November 2026 the menu consists of 13 starters, 3 salads, 8 warm small plates, 11 main courses and 5 desserts; in the morning there is a serpme breakfast and Brioche Benedict. Guests staying at Didi Otel are served breakfast at Mare Gastro as a plated breakfast; the serpme breakfast can be chosen for an extra charge. Guests with children are welcome until 15:00 (breakfast hours); the children's menu (Grilled Meatballs) is valid only during those hours.

Current dishes and prices are on the digital menu. For breakfast details, see our breakfast post.

Planning Accommodation and Dinner Together

For those who want to stay the night instead of driving back to Istanbul after dinner, the easiest way is Didi Otel in the same garden. On special evenings such as birthdays, anniversaries or marriage proposals, planning dinner and accommodation together removes the worry of transport. We described this in detail in our post on dinner and accommodation together.

We gathered daytime options for families with children in our breakfast with kids guide; after 15:00 the restaurant serves adult guests only.

Commonly Confused Points

  • Reservations are separate: Room reservations are made with Didi Otel, table reservations with Mare Gastro.
  • The menu is the same for everyone: The same à la carte menu applies to hotel guests and outside guests.
  • Monday: The restaurant is closed on Mondays; for the hotel's own schedule, ask Didi Otel.

Frequently Asked Questions

Mare Gastro · Sapanca
Which is Didi Otel's restaurant?
The restaurant in the garden of Didi Otel is Mare Gastro. Right next to the lake, it serves Chef Doğan Anapa's Aegean and Mediterranean cuisine à la carte.
Where in Didi Otel is Mare Gastro?
In the hotel's garden; it shares the same entrance and parking lot as the hotel. Address: Kırkpınar Soğuksu, İstasyon 3 Cad. No:55, Sapanca/Sakarya.
Can you eat at Mare Gastro without staying at the hotel?
Yes. Mare Gastro is open to everyone; you can book and come without being a hotel guest. Parking is free.
Which days is Mare Gastro open?
Open 09:00–00:00 Tuesday to Sunday; closed on Mondays.
Is breakfast at Didi Otel served at Mare Gastro?
Yes. Guests staying at Didi Otel are served breakfast at Mare Gastro as a plated breakfast; the serpme breakfast can be chosen for an extra charge. The breakfast menu is open to everyone from 09:00 Tuesday to Sunday.
