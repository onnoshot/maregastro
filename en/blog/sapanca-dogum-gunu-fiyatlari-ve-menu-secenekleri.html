Birthday Celebration Prices and Menu Options in Sapanca

Last updated: November 16, 20268 min readMare Gastro

Short answer: What a birthday celebration costs in Sapanca varies with the number of guests, the dishes chosen, the drinks and any cake/decoration request; there is no fixed package price. At Mare Gastro you order from the à la carte menu, current dish-by-dish prices are on the digital menu, and cake is a separate item in the Extras section.

How Much Does a Birthday Celebration Cost in Sapanca?

Giving a single figure would be misleading: at one table two people can have a meze-led evening while at another ten people celebrate with main courses, wine and cake. That is why we leave the price not to this post but to the digital menu, which is always current. The tables below show how you can plan the budget.

What Sets the Price?

  • Number of guests: The factor that affects the total most.
  • Choice of dishes: Main courses built around seafood and red meat raise the bill.
  • Drinks: Wine, rakı and cocktails are charged separately.
  • Cake and decoration: Cake is a separate item under Extras; decoration requests are settled in the reservation.
  • Season: The menu is updated with the season.

How Are Cake and Decoration Planned?

When you reserve, you pass on the number of guests, the date and your request for cake, candles or table decoration; the details are settled with the team. If it is to be a surprise, mention that in the note too: the cake arrives at the table at the right moment without the birthday guest knowing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Mare Gastro · Sapanca
How much does a birthday celebration cost in Sapanca?
It varies with the number of guests, the dishes chosen, the drinks and the cake request. Mare Gastro has an à la carte menu; current dish prices are on the digital menu at maregastro.com/menu.
Does Mare Gastro have a birthday package?
No. Instead of a fixed package you choose from the à la carte menu; cake and decoration requests are planned when you reserve.
Can you order cake at Mare Gastro?
Yes. Cake is a separate item in the Extras section of the digital menu; just mention it is a birthday in the reservation.
Are drinks included in the price?
No. Drinks are chosen separately from the menu and charged separately.
Can you have a birthday evening with children?
Mare Gastro welcomes guests with children until 15:00 (breakfast hours); children are not admitted after 15:00. You can plan a celebration with children during the day; the children's menu has Grilled Meatballs.
How can I find out current prices?
You can see item-by-item prices on the digital menu, and for large group plans talk to the team through the reservation form or WhatsApp.
