Birthday Celebration Prices and Menu Options in Sapanca

Updated 2026-10-247 min readMare Gastro

When budgeting a birthday in Sapanca, weigh what a venue offers alongside its price. Here is Mare Gastro's menu structure, dishes suited to a celebration table and how booking works. Updated 24 October 2026.

Quick Answer: How Much Does a Birthday Dinner Cost in Sapanca?

It depends on the number of guests, the dishes, drinks and any cake or decoration requests. Mare Gastro has no fixed birthday package: you order from the à la carte menu, and current dish-by-dish prices are on the digital menu. Cake and decoration are planned separately when you book.

For larger tables, ordering starters and warm plates to share and choosing mains individually balances both the budget and the flow of the evening.

What Determines the Price?

  • Number of guests: the biggest factor in the total.
  • Dish choice: seafood and red meat mains raise the bill.
  • Drinks: wine, rakı and cocktails are charged separately.
  • Cake and decoration: confirmed separately when booking.
  • Season: the menu is updated with the seasons.

How Are the Cake and Decoration Arranged?

When booking, share the number of guests, the date and your cake or table decoration request; the details are finalised with the team. Guest reviews mention this care:
