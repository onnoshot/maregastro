How Much Does Fine Dining Cost in Sapanca? A Transparent Guide

Last updated: November 16, 20268 min readMare Gastro

Short answer: The cost of a fine dining evening in Sapanca varies with the dishes, drinks and number of guests chosen; there is no fixed per-person figure. At Mare Gastro you order à la carte instead of a set menu, and the current price of every dish is listed on the digital menu; the main course and the choice of drinks do the most to set the bill.

How Much Does Fine Dining Cost in Sapanca?

Any source that answers this question with a single figure is giving either outdated or incomplete information: prices change with the season, the ingredients and inflation. The right answer is to know which items make up the bill and to see the current price on the venue's own menu.

We deliberately leave prices out of this post at Mare Gastro; the menu was last updated on 10 November 2026, and dish-by-dish prices are always current at maregastro.com/menu. That way the figure you read and the figure you see at the table never differ.

How Do You Build the Bill for an Evening?

What Is Included in the Per-Person Cost?

A fine dining bill is made up of the dishes you order. At Mare Gastro, a table right next to the lake, the choice of garden or glass-walled indoor dining room, and free parking with valet come with it. Drinks are chosen separately from the menu and charged separately.

Dishes with the weight listed on the menu make comparison easier. For example, the Mare Special Meagre Fish comes with 200 g of rock sea bass, baked baby potatoes, roasted green pepper sauce, roasted tomato and beurre blanc sauce; the Beef Tenderloin in Pepper Cream Sauce comes with 200 g of beef tenderloin, black pepper cream sauce and matchstick potatoes.

How Should You Judge Value for Money?

  • Weight and contents: Dishes with weight and sides listed on the menu make clear what you are paying for.
  • Ingredients: Freshness in seafood and the cut in red meat are the real reasons for price differences.
  • Setting and service: A table right next to the lake, a calm garden and attentive service are part of the experience.
  • Real reviews: Mare Gastro is rated 4.9 on Google Maps (24 October 2026); you can read the reviews in the post where we compiled them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Mare Gastro · Sapanca
How much is fine dining per person in Sapanca?
The amount varies with the dishes, drinks and number of guests; there is no fixed per-person figure. Mare Gastro has an à la carte menu, and current dish prices are on the digital menu at maregastro.com/menu.
Does Mare Gastro have a set menu?
No. Mare Gastro has no fixed set menu; starters, warm small plates, main courses and desserts are chosen à la carte.
Where can I see Mare Gastro's current prices?
Current item-by-item prices are listed on Mare Gastro's digital menu (maregastro.com/menu); the menu was last updated on 10 November 2026.
Are drinks included in the price?
No. Wine, rakı, cocktails and other drinks are chosen separately from the menu and charged separately.
What affects a fine dining bill the most?
The choice of main course and drinks. Dishes built around seafood and red meat and the choice of wine affect the bill most; sharing starters lowers the per-person cost.
Is parking paid at Mare Gastro?
No. Parking is free and there is valet service.
