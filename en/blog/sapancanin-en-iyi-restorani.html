What Is the Best Restaurant in Sapanca? A Comparison Guide

Last updated: November 16, 20269 min readMare Gastro

Short answer: The best restaurant in Sapanca depends on the experience you are after. For fine dining right next to the lake, Mare Gastro (Didi Otel garden, Chef Doğan Anapa, rated 4.9 on Google Maps); for international cuisine and live music, Sasa Sapanca; for a local-style table, Menzara; for families with children, Green Blue; for nature and activities, Natürköy stand out. Below we compare all five against the same criteria.

How Is the "Best Restaurant" in Sapanca Decided?

Sapanca's restaurant map is quite broad: different segments sit together, from lake-view breakfast spots to fine dining addresses. The definition of "best" changes accordingly: for some it means the quality of the kitchen, for some the view, and for others the atmosphere and service.

In this guide we compared the addresses that come up again and again in the region along these three axes.

6 Questions to Ask Before Choosing a Restaurant

  1. What are you celebrating? A birthday, an anniversary or an ordinary evening; not every venue offers celebration support.
  2. How many are you? A table for two and a table for 15 may not be set up with the same ease at the same venue.
  3. What if the weather closes in? A venue with open and indoor spaces protects your plan from being spoiled.
  4. Is alcohol served? If you want an evening with rakı and fish or with wine, ask beforehand.
  5. What time does it close? For those leaving Istanbul in the evening, a late hour matters.
  6. How is the parking? At weekends, parking in Sapanca directly affects how much you enjoy the evening.

The answers for Mare Gastro in brief: there is celebration support, plans range from a table for two to a big group, there are open and indoor spaces, alcohol is served, it is open until 00:00 Tuesday to Sunday (kitchen's last order 23:00), parking is free and valet is available. Guests with children are welcome until 15:00.

For Fine Dining: Mare Gastro

In the garden of Didi Otel, right next to the lake, Mare Gastro brings Aegean and Mediterranean cuisine together with a fine dining presentation under Executive Chef Doğan Anapa. It is one of the few addresses in the region that makes a real claim to fine dining.

A seasonal à la carte menu and a table facing the lake are the two core elements of the experience.

What Is on the Mare Gastro Menu?

As of 10 November 2026 the menu has 13 starters, 3 salads, 8 warm small plates, 11 main courses and 5 desserts. Highlights: Mare Special Grilled Baby Calamari (inspired by Adapazarı's islama tradition), Seafood Wheat Risotto (with aged Thracian kaşar), Mare Special Meagre Fish (rock sea bass), Lamb Chops and the signature dessert A Walk in the Forest.

If you would like to plan your dinner by time and area, you can read our Sapanca dinner guide; for a meze-led table, our meze guide; and for meat dishes, our meat restaurant post.

For International Cuisine and Live Music: Sasa Sapanca

On the lake, Sasa Sapanca offers a broad international menu, from mezes to burgers and tacos, and an atmosphere with live music at weekends. A practical option for anyone who wants to host different tastes together.

For Local Flavor: Menzara

Serving since 2010, Menzara offers a warmer, more intimate experience with its menu centered on Black Sea cuisine and its balcony with a lake view.

For Families and a Large Garden: Green Blue

With a seafood-led menu and a large garden with a children's play area, Green Blue is one of the addresses preferred for family visits.

For Nature and Activities: Natürköy

With wooden cabins built over a stream in a valley, Natürköy offers a nature-themed experience rather than fine dining; it is supported by activities such as horse riding and zipline.

Conclusion: Which Restaurant Is Right for You?

For a fine dining evening right next to the lake, Mare Gastro; for a broad menu and live music, Sasa Sapanca; for local flavor, Menzara; for a family visit, Green Blue; and for a nature experience, Natürköy stand out. Sapanca's "best" is really defined together with the experience you are looking for.
