Luxury Restaurant in Sapanca: Fine Dining at Mare Gastro

Last updated: November 16, 20267 min readMare Gastro

Short answer: For a luxurious dinner in Sapanca, Mare Gastro offers fine dining in the garden of Didi Otel, right next to the lake: Aegean and Mediterranean cuisine by Executive Chef Doğan Anapa, an open garden and a glass-walled indoor dining room, free parking and valet. Open 09:00–00:00 Tuesday to Sunday; rated 4.9 on Google Maps (24 October 2026).

What Defines a Luxury Restaurant in Sapanca?

The word "luxury" is often matched only with decor or price. Yet the things that make a guest remember an evening are more concrete: the signature of the kitchen, the attention of the service, the position of the table and small details thought through in advance.

A Garden Right Next to the Lake, a Glass-Walled Dining Room

Mare Gastro is in the garden of Didi Otel, surrounded by palms and pines. On summer evenings you sit in the garden, and on cool days in the indoor dining room where the pool's light falls on the glass. The light and calm of the evening are what set this place apart from Sapanca's crowded coast road.

You can see all the photos of the venue in our photo gallery; daily posts are on our Instagram and TikTok accounts.

What Is in the Kitchen?

The menu is à la carte and changes with the season. As of 10 November 2026 there are 13 starters, 3 salads, 8 warm small plates, 11 main courses and 5 desserts. Frequent choices for a luxurious evening:

  • Jumbo Shrimp Salad with Celery Purée — orange celery purée, green apple
  • Bonito Carpaccio — garlic vinaigrette, capers, dill
  • Mare Special Meagre Fish — 200 g rock sea bass, roasted green pepper sauce, beurre blanc
  • Beef Tenderloin in Pepper Cream Sauce — 200 g beef tenderloin, black pepper cream sauce
  • A Walk in the Forest — chocolate mousse, crispy profiterole, vanilla ice cream

All the dishes and current prices are on the digital menu.

Who Is Behind the Concept?

From the lighting to the table layout, Mare Gastro's concept was designed by Kadir Çiçek within Didi Otel. In the kitchen is Executive Chef Doğan Anapa, who carries his cinema background onto the plate. The whole story is in our post on the story of Mare Gastro.

Practical Tips for a Luxurious Evening

  • For weekend evenings, make a reservation a few days ahead.
  • If it is a special occasion (birthday, anniversary, proposal), leave a note in the reservation.
  • There is no dress code; a relaxed-smart choice suits the evening. Details are in our post on fine dining etiquette.
  • If you don't want to drive back to Istanbul at night, you can stay at Didi Otel in the same garden.

How Are Special Occasions Prepared?

For evenings such as birthdays, anniversaries or marriage proposals, cake, candles, flowers and table decoration are planned through the reservation note; cake is a separate item in the Extras section of the digital menu. If you are planning a proposal, we explained it in detail in our post on marriage proposals, and for anniversaries in our anniversary guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the luxury restaurant in Sapanca?
Mare Gastro is a fine dining restaurant in the Kırkpınar area of Sapanca, in the garden of Didi Otel and right next to Lake Sapanca.
Which days is Mare Gastro open?
