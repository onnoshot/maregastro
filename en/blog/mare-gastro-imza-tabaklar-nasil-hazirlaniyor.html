How Are Mare Gastro's Signature Plates Made?

2026-11-078 min readMare Gastro

Short answer: Mare Gastro's signature plates come from Executive Chef Doğan Anapa's way of interpreting the local flavours of Sakarya with Aegean-Mediterranean technique. Islama calamari is inspired by Adapazarı's ıslama köfte, and lamb shank with keşkek by the region's keşkek tradition. Below we summarise six plates in the chef's own words from his videos.

Mare Special Islama Calamari

In the chef's words, the idea for the plate comes from Adapazarı's famous ıslama köfte: where the bread for ıslama köfte is soaked in bone broth, here it is soaked in fish stock. Grilled baby squid is placed on bread soaked in fish stock and turned in sweet paprika and butter.

It is finished with the chef's own mint and mustard sauce, and diced tomato and spring onion for freshness and acidity. As of November 2026 it is on the menu among the hot appetisers as “Mare Special Islama Calamari”.

Seafood Wheat Risotto

Instead of rice, whole wheat berries are used. The wheat is cooked slowly with small shrimp and pieces of baby squid in garlic, parsley and butter and finished with white wine. On top go separately cooked shell-on jumbo prawns and clams.

The chef's choice: the prawns are cooked just enough to stay slightly firm, and instead of parmesan the dish is finished with a local cheese, aged Thracian kaşar. It is on the menu among the main courses.

Lamb Kokoreç on a Bed of Watermelon Salad

To balance the richness of the kokoreç, the plate sits on a salad of tomato, watermelon and basil with a touch of vinegar. On top come pieces of grilled lavash, then kokoreç that is first cooked slowly in a 160 °C oven and then turned in butter on the grill.

It is seasoned with cumin, chilli flakes, thyme and salt and served with roasted green pepper. Lamb Kokoreç is on the menu among the main courses.

Grilled Shell-On Tiger Prawns

The tiger prawns are split in half and cooked in the shell; according to the chef, prawns gain much more flavour inside their shell. The sauce is simple: garlic, butter, salt and parsley. They are served with lime and lemon.

In the video the recipe is prepared together with Kadir Çiçek, Chairman of the Board of Didi Otel. On the menu you'll find “Grilled Jumbo Prawns” and “Prawns in Butter” among the hot appetisers.

Lamb Shank on Keşkek

The lamb shank is cooked slowly in a cast-iron pot in the oven at 180 °C for about 2.5–3 hours, with onion, garlic, dried herbs such as thyme, mint and basil, stock and various acids. The keşkek is made with wheat, chickpeas, onion, chicken and butter; as the chef points out, keşkek is a much-loved dish in Adapazarı.

The plate is finished with the shank's own sauce and mint oil. This dish was on the summer menu; since the menu changes with the season, check the current menu.

Rock Sea Bass and Karasu Asparagus

The thick rock sea bass first goes on the grill and then continues cooking in the oven. Under the fish are two “hidden sauces”: one with roasted green pepper, garlic and fennel, and one with vinegar and tarragon. Alongside are oven-roasted potatoes and tomatoes, and on top fresh asparagus from Karasu, basil, pickled onion and lemon.

This plate was also on the summer menu; today you can check the menu for the current fish dishes.

What Do These Plates Have in Common?

Three common threads: local produce (Karasu asparagus, aged Thracian kaşar), references to the region's food culture (ıslama, keşkek) and a balance of acidity (vinegar-based sauces, mint oil, tomato). We describe the chef's approach in more detail in Who is Chef Doğan Anapa?.
