A Bachelorette Dinner in Sapanca: A Planning Guide

November 16, 20267 min readMare Gastro

Short answer: At about 1.5 hours from Istanbul, Sapanca suits groups who want to leave the city for a bachelorette. At Mare Gastro the group table, table decoration and cake are planned through the reservation; the menu has 12 alcoholic cocktails and most weekends there is live music. Staying the night at Didi Otel in the same garden removes the worry of getting home.

Why Sapanca for a Bachelorette?

In Istanbul, a bachelorette often comes down to a crowded venue, traffic and a midnight trip home. Sapanca is a distance that feels like leaving the city without being tiring: about 1.5 hours on the road, dinner right next to the lake and, for those who want it, an overnight stay in the same garden.

How Do You Plan a Bachelorette Dinner? 6 Steps

  1. Settle the number of guests. The table layout and the choice of space depend on it; for groups of 8 or more write a few days ahead, and for weekends one to two weeks ahead.
  2. Decide whether the bride will know. If it is to be a surprise, make the reservation in the organizer's name, not the bride's, and say so in the note.
  3. Ask for decoration and cake in the reservation. Cake is a separate item in the Extras section of the digital menu; the details of table decoration are settled with the team.
  4. Discuss the organization details with the restaurant. The rules for organization, such as decoration, accessories, music or surprises, are set separately for each event; contact the restaurant and talk through your plan beforehand.
  5. Build the menu for sharing. Starters and warm small plates for the middle of the table, main courses per person.
  6. Plan the way home from the start. If you will be drinking, arrange an overnight stay or a driver.

Small Surprises for the Bride

  • A memory book: A small notebook in which each friend writes the bride a note makes the most lasting gift of the evening.
  • A group photo: In the garden, at sunset.
  • A note on the cake: A short message on top of the cake; cake is a separate item under Extras.
  • Video messages: Short videos collected in advance from friends who can't come, watched on a phone at the table.

What to Eat and Drink at the Table

The arrangement that works best at a bachelorette dinner is small plates shared over a long time. A setup often chosen at Mare Gastro: start with Crete-Style Cheese Spread, Hummus, Beetroot Borani and Spicy Atom with Potato Chips, continue with the Trio of Seafood Delights, and let everyone choose their own main course.

How Do You Work Out the Budget?

There is no fixed bachelorette package at Mare Gastro; you order from the à la carte menu. The items that affect the budget most are the number of guests, the drinks (especially sparkling wine and cocktails) and the choice of main course. Current prices are on the digital menu; if you would like a rough plan for your group, you can talk to the team on WhatsApp.

Accommodation: Where Do You Stay at the End of the Night?

The most comfortable version of an out-of-town bachelorette with a group is staying where the dinner is. Mare Gastro is in the garden of Didi Otel; for room and accommodation information you can contact the hotel, and you can mention in the reservation note that you will be staying over.

You can find other tips for large groups in our group dinner post and options by type of celebration in our special occasion guide.

What Do You Wear to a Bachelorette Dinner?

Mare Gastro has no strict dress code; a relaxed-smart choice is enough. If you will be sitting in the garden, a wedge or flat shoe instead of heels and a shawl or jacket for the evening chill help. If you have chosen theme colors, share that in the reservation note too; the table decoration can be prepared in the same colors.

For Those Who Don't Drink

Every group may have someone who doesn't drink alcohol. The menu has non-alcoholic cocktails, milkshakes, frozen drinks, iced coffee and cold drinks; it is a good idea to discuss them beforehand so that everyone has a glass in hand at the toast.

A Daytime Alternative: A Bachelorette Breakfast

For groups who would rather celebrate in the daytime than in the evening, Mare Gastro opens at 09:00 with a serpme breakfast. A long breakfast in the garden in daylight also gives the best light for photos, and the group can then explore Sapanca for the rest of the day. We described the breakfast menu in our breakfast post.
