Open Late

A Late Dinner in Sapanca: A Restaurant Open Until Midnight

Last updated: November 16, 20266 min readMare Gastro

Short answer: For a late dinner in Sapanca, Mare Gastro in the garden of Didi Otel is open until 00:00 Tuesday to Sunday; the kitchen takes its last order at 23:00, and it is closed on Mondays. The restaurant does not stay open around the clock: it closes at midnight. The evening service has the chef's à la carte menu, a cocktail and wine list, and most weekends live music.

Where Can You Eat a Late Dinner in Sapanca?

A good part of Sapanca's restaurants work around breakfast and the daytime; as the evening goes on, the options thin out. Mare Gastro works in a pattern that opens with breakfast at 09:00 and closes at midnight; the kitchen takes last orders until 23:00. For people setting off from Istanbul after work, this means being able to eat at a table around 21:00–22:00 without rushing.

The kitchen's last order is 23:00. If you will be arriving after 22:00, write your arrival time in the reservation note; main courses need to be ordered before 23:00.

What Do You Eat and Drink Late?

The arrangement most often seen at a late table is to open with a few shared starters and a warm small plate and continue with a main course. Warm small plates such as Mare Special Grilled Baby Calamari, octopus casserole and jumbo shrimp suit a late table well; for those who don't want a heavy dinner, starters such as Hummus, Crete-Style Cheese Spread or Beetroot Borani may be enough.

On the drinks side there are rakı, wine, champagne and signature cocktails; the detailed list is in our post on alcohol-serving restaurants. If you are planning a long night with rakı, you can read our rakı and fish guide.

A Night Venue, or a Restaurant Open Until Late?

A search for "Sapanca night venues" usually covers two different wishes: a restaurant where you can sit until late, or an entertainment venue with music. Mare Gastro is the first: not a nightclub, but a restaurant that closes at midnight and serves dinner with live music. The music is kept at a level that doesn't drown out conversation.

You can follow the weekend live music program in our live music post; one of the past performances is below.

Where Do You Stay After a Late Dinner?

Instead of driving back to Istanbul at midnight, staying at Didi Otel in the same garden is the most practical option; your car stays in the free parking lot. You can state your accommodation preference in the reservation.

Should I Reserve for the Evening?

On weeknights a table can usually be found; on Friday and Saturday nights, and on evenings with live music, tables fill faster. Just choose the date and time in the reservation form and write your arrival time as a note.

On weeknights traffic usually flows better; at weekends, the journey can take longer when you leave on a Friday evening. For the live time, use the route on the Mare Gastro directions page.

4 Tips for a Late Dinner

  1. Write your arrival time in the reservation. The kitchen's last order is 23:00; for late arrivals, the table and the kitchen are prepared accordingly.
  2. Open with shared starters. Instead of a heavy main course late at night, a few mezes and a warm small plate can be enough.
  3. Think about the way home from the start. If you will be drinking, plan a driver or an overnight stay.
  4. Bring a jacket. If you will be sitting in the garden, nights around the lake cool down.
