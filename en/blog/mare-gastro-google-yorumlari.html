Mare Gastro Google Reviews: What Our Guests Say

2026-08-265 min readMare Gastro

The most honest introduction to a restaurant is not the story it tells about itself but what guests write on Google after they leave the table. We looked at Mare Gastro's reviews: real experiences, shared under real names, about the atmosphere, the service team and special-day celebrations. The reviews were written in Turkish; below we quote them in translation.

Why Look at Google Reviews?

A venue's photos, menu and its own story are always presented at their best, and that's normal. Google reviews do something different: they leave a record outside the brand's control, written voluntarily by a guest after the meal. When you are looking for a fine dining evening or a place for a special celebration in Sapanca, these reviews are the most reliable way to see whether a restaurant's promise matches what it actually delivers.

In this article we have gathered real examples from the reviews guests posted with their own accounts on Mare Gastro's Google Maps page. No name or text has been made up. Mare Gastro is rated 4.9 on Google Maps (October 2026).

The 3 Things Guests Mention Most

When you put dozens of reviews side by side, three themes keep coming back:

  • Atmosphere and setting: the open-air veranda, the natural surroundings right next to the lake and evenings with live music.
  • The service team: a team that guests thank by name in their reviews, for its personal attention.
  • Special occasions: birthdays and wedding anniversaries, once-in-a-lifetime moments, handled with care.

“A very pleasant and peaceful place, outdoors and close to nature. The friendly smile and kind manner of Muhammed Bey, who looked after us, made our experience even better.”

Ümmühan Tahta · Google review, translated from Turkish

What Guests Say About Birthday and Anniversary Celebrations

One phrase appears in the reviews far more often than any other: “we came for our anniversary”. Fikriye Erkan, Furkan Kavakcı and Büşra Canbazoğlu all wrote about anniversary evenings:

“I found it by chance and came after reading the reviews, and I'm so glad I did! The food, the service and the ambience are excellent, and the live DJ music was wonderful too. We came for our wedding anniversary and I didn't expect this much. Special thanks to Süleyman Bey, who looked after us.”

Fikriye Erkan · Google review, translated from Turkish

“We went with my wife for our anniversary. The atmosphere and the service are truly wonderful. You definitely have to try the flavour of the dishes and the aroma of the meze. I'll certainly be back soon.”

Furkan Kavakcı · Google review, translated from Turkish

“Even though it was our first visit, we fell in love with the atmosphere. This place made our wedding anniversary unforgettable: the setting, presentation, service and music were flawless. The food arrives at your table as a work of art.”

Büşra Canbazoğlu · Google review, translated from Turkish

“A very stylish and refined place; we chose it for our anniversary and were very happy. Special thanks to the service team. I recommend the lamb shank.”

Mert Efe Demir · Google review, translated from Turkish

The picture is similar for birthdays:

“We came for dinner for my wife's birthday. Being at the table looked after by our waiter Muhammed added as much to our evening as the food did. We chose the chef's interpretation dishes and don't regret it at all.”

Ardata Girgin · Google review, translated from Turkish

It shows that Mare Gastro is more than a place to eat: it is an address where the special day itself is designed.

The Service Team Is Named Again and Again

One of the clearest signs that a restaurant is really good is whether guests mention staff by name. In Mare Gastro's reviews these names come up again and again: Muhammed Bey is praised for his attention at the welcome and with bookings, Süleyman Bey accompanies guests on special-day celebrations, and Ömer Bey is thanked for explaining the menu dish by dish and answering every question throughout the evening. That's no coincidence: personal attention to every guest is at the heart of Mare Gastro's service.

“From the welcome to the very last moment, it makes you feel special and crowns the night with a feast for the eyes and the palate. Well done to everyone.”

Yasemin Babadağ · Google review, translated from Turkish

“We came by chance, but both the ambience and the food were excellent. Our waiter Ömer Bey was extremely attentive all evening. If we're ever in Sapanca again, we'll definitely be back.”

İnci Ogan · Google review, translated from Turkish

Behind the Kitchen and the Concept: Chef Doğan Anapa and Kadir Çiçek

Two names stand behind the atmosphere and flavours praised in the reviews. Mare Gastro was created as a concept designed by Kadir Çiçek, with the idea of adding a fine dining experience to the lake's natural setting without disturbing it. At the head of the kitchen is Executive Chef Doğan Anapa, who brought his path from film to the kitchen into the simple, ingredient-led approach of Aegean and Mediterranean cuisine. Phrases that appear often in the reviews, such as “the aroma of the meze”, “beef carpaccio”, “lamb shank” and “the chef's interpretation dishes”, show how this philosophy reaches the table.
