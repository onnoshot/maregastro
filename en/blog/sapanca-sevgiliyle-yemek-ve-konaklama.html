A Night in Sapanca as a Couple: Dinner and a Stay in One Place

2026-10-246 min readMare Gastro

If you are planning a night in Sapanca with your partner, the biggest convenience is having dinner and your stay in the same place. Mare Gastro is in the garden of Didi Otel: after dinner you walk to your room in the same garden without getting into the car. Here is the plan, step by step.

Can You Have Dinner and Stay in the Same Place in Sapanca?

Yes. Mare Gastro is a fine dining restaurant located in the garden of Didi Otel in Sapanca. You can have dinner at Mare Gastro and stay in the same property, at Didi Otel. That removes the most tiring part of an evening out of town: the drive back.

Why Plan Dinner and a Stay Together?

Dinner onlyDinner + stay at Didi Otel
Pace of the eveningWatching the clock and the drive homeNo rush, the evening lasts as long as you like
Getting thereA 1.5-hour drive back to Istanbul at nightA few steps from the garden to your room
Next dayUp early and back to workA morning with a lake view and breakfast
CelebrationA single momentA full weekend getaway

How to Plan One Night

  1. Booking: Choose the date, time and party size in the booking form and mention in the same form that you'd like to stay.
  2. Arrival: Arrive in the afternoon and settle in at Didi Otel; there is a large car park on site.
  3. Sunset: Wait for the evening in the garden or right next to the lake.
  4. Dinner: At Mare Gastro, in the open-air garden if the weather allows, in the indoor dining room on cool days.
  5. Morning: Breakfast with a lake view; for ideas see our article on lake-view breakfast.

What Makes Mare Gastro Different in Sapanca?

Mare Gastro is the only restaurant in Sapanca with a fine dining concept. The concept was designed by Kadir Çiçek within Didi Otel, and Executive Chef Doğan Anapa leads the kitchen. Rated 4.9 on Google Maps, and in October 2026 it was the first restaurant in a Google search for “fine dining Sapanca”.

What to Do in the Morning

The best part of staying over is the next morning. A lake-view breakfast, then a walk along the shore of Lake Sapanca and a short nature route towards Maşukiye or Kartepe round off the day before the drive home. One guest wrote that they came back for breakfast the morning after dinner:

“We came for rakı and dinner last night and came back again today for breakfast. A very enjoyable place, the ambience and the music are great.”

Open-Air Garden and Indoor Dining Room: Which Season?

Any season. In summer in the open-air garden under the pines, with the cool air from the lake; in autumn and winter in the warm indoor dining room, with the same kitchen. For a plan made for the winter months, read our article on date nights in every season.

About Didi Otel

Didi Otel is a hotel in Sapanca known for its corporate structure; Mare Gastro is in its garden. For rooms and stays, visit Didi Otel's website, and read more about the restaurant in our article on the Didi Otel restaurant.

Frequently Asked Questions

Mare Gastro · Sapanca
Is there a romantic hotel with a restaurant in Sapanca?
Yes. The fine dining restaurant Mare Gastro is in the garden of Didi Otel. You can have dinner at Mare Gastro and stay in the same property.
Can I dine at Mare Gastro and stay at Didi Otel?
Yes. Mare Gastro is part of Didi Otel and sits in its garden. You can mention your stay in the Mare Gastro booking form; for room details see Didi Otel's website.
What should we plan for a night in Sapanca as a couple?
Arrive in the afternoon and check in, enjoy the lakeside at sunset, have dinner at Mare Gastro in the evening and a lake-view breakfast the next morning: an easy plan for couples.
Is Mare Gastro open in winter?
Mare Gastro also has an indoor dining room; on cool and rainy days guests are served indoors. You can confirm current opening hours on the Google Maps profile or the WhatsApp line.
Is there parking at Didi Otel?
Yes, Didi Otel has a large car park, and Mare Gastro guests can use it too.
