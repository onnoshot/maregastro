Breakfast with Kids in Sapanca: A Daytime Guide for Families

November 16, 20267 min readMare Gastro

Short answer: Mare Gastro welcomes guests with children until 15:00, that is, during breakfast and lunch hours; after 15:00 the restaurant serves adult guests only. For families visiting Sapanca with kids, the best plan is breakfast, which starts at 09:00 Tuesday to Sunday: Turkish spread breakfast (serpme), Brioche Benedict, Grilled Meatballs and snacks for children, an open garden and free parking.

What Time Can You Come to Mare Gastro with Children?

The time rule is clear: families with children are welcome between 09:00 and 15:00. After 15:00 the restaurant serves adult guests only, so a dinner plan with children does not suit Mare Gastro. Getting the plan right from the start is the easiest way to avoid disappointment at the door.

When you reserve, mention in the note that you are coming with children and your time; the table is arranged accordingly, in daylight and close to the garden.

What Is on the Table for Breakfast with Kids?

The backbone of the breakfast menu is the serpme breakfast. According to the menu dated 10 November 2026, it includes black and grilled green olives, apple-cinnamon and strawberry jam, acuka, flower honey, clotted cream, butter, fried eggplant and peppers with tomato sauce, Ezine cheese, fresh kaşar, çeçil cheese, a plate of fresh vegetables, gözleme, pişi, french fries, sucuk fried in the pan, omelet or menemen, muska börek, melted cheese in a clay pot, seasonal fruit and tea. Pişi, gözleme, fries and omelet, which most children like, are already part of the table.

What About Breakfast for Families Staying at Didi Otel?

Guests staying at Didi Otel are served breakfast as a plated breakfast at Mare Gastro in the same garden. Hotel guests who want the serpme breakfast can choose it for an extra charge; the current price is on the digital menu. For families with children this means starting the morning in the garden without leaving the hotel. The accommodation side is covered in Didi Otel's guide to a family holiday in Sapanca (in Turkish).

What Is the Best Time for Breakfast with Kids?

Weekend breakfast is the most in-demand meal in Sapanca; writing to the reservation form a few days ahead for Saturday and Sunday makes the morning easier.

What to Bring When Going to Breakfast with Kids

  • Wet wipes, a change of clothes and a bib
  • The spoon and cup your child is used to
  • A quiet toy or a book
  • A light cardigan for the morning chill; mornings around the lake can be cool

Safety: Mind the Pool Area

There is Didi Otel's pool in the garden; always keep small children under your supervision. Not running between tables and letting kids watch videos with headphones makes the morning easier for you and for the neighboring tables. When a child gets bored, a short walk in the garden is usually enough.

What to Do About Dinner with Children?

Because evenings at Mare Gastro (after 15:00) are reserved for adults, for dinner with children you need to look at family-oriented restaurants in Sapanca. There are places in the area known for a big garden and a children's play area; Green Blue, for example, stands out for these features. We made the comparison in our post on the best restaurants in Sapanca; confirm the children's rules with each venue before you go.

If you want to celebrate your child's birthday at Mare Gastro, plan the celebration for the daytime, before 15:00. Cake is a separate item in the Extras section of the digital menu; decoration and other organization details are discussed by contacting the restaurant.

No. After 15:00 the restaurant serves adult guests only.
