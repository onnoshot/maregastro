A Luxury Gastronomy Getaway Near Istanbul: Sapanca

2026-06-056 min readMare Gastro

An escape from the city's noise to a gourmet retreat: Sapanca, only 1.5 hours from Istanbul, is one of the nearest natural havens for a luxury dinner.

Close to the City, Open to Nature

It's possible to step into a completely different world shortly after leaving Istanbul. About 1.5 hours away, Sapanca offers an ideal route for those looking for a luxury restaurant near Istanbul. The lake, forest and calm become the foundation of a weekend escape.

The gastronomic stop of this route is Mare Gastro — fine dining right by the lake, in the garden of Didi Otel.

A Day Trip or a Weekend?

Sapanca suits both a single-day getaway and a weekend plan. A walk in nature by day, a gourmet table at Mare Gastro by evening — a balanced, unforgettable program.

Those wishing to extend the evening can stay at Didi Otel; simply note your accommodation preference in the reservation form.

What Does a Luxury Dinner Deserve?

An evening worth the trip is the flawless meeting of view, service and flavor. The pure tastes of the Aegean and Mediterranean arrive at your table through Executive Chef Doğan Anapa's interpretation. Explore the menu.

Plan Ahead

We recommend reserving in advance to secure your spot during busy weekends; sunset hours fill up especially fast.
