How to Get to Mare Gastro: Directions, Parking and Valet

Last updated: November 16, 20266 min readMare Gastro

Short answer: Mare Gastro is in the Kırkpınar Soğuksu neighborhood of Sapanca, in the garden of Didi Otel and right next to Lake Sapanca (İstasyon 3 Cad. No:55). It is about 1.5 hours from Istanbul by car; just type "Mare Gastro" into Google Maps or tap one of the links below. Parking is free and valet service is available.

Where Exactly Is Mare Gastro?

The restaurant is in the garden of Didi Otel; the hotel and the restaurant share the same entrance and the same parking lot. Full address: Kırkpınar Soğuksu, İstasyon 3 Cad. No:55, 54600 Sapanca/Sakarya. Searching "Mare Gastro" or "Didi Otel Sapanca" in your navigation app leads to the same point.

Where Do You Park When You Come by Car?

Didi Otel's parking lot is free for restaurant guests. There is valet service at the entrance; you can leave the key and go straight to your table. If you plan to drink at dinner, the most comfortable option is to leave the car in the lot and stay the night at Didi Otel in the same garden.

We have gathered all the questions about valet and parking in our post on restaurants with valet and parking in Sapanca.

Is It Possible to Come by Train or Taxi?

Yes. Sapanca can also be reached by train; we recommend checking with TCDD Taşımacılık which services stop at Sapanca or at nearby Arifiye station, and the current times. From the station you take a taxi to the restaurant; for the return, you can ask the team to help with a taxi.

If you are coming as a group and want to reduce the number of cars, write it in the reservation note; parking and welcome will be planned accordingly.

What to Know Before You Come

  • We are closed on Mondays. We are open 09:00–00:00 Tuesday to Sunday.
  • Book for weekends. On Friday and Saturday evenings tables fill faster because of the live music on most weekends.
  • Leave the special-occasion note in advance. For a birthday, an anniversary or a marriage proposal, cake, candles and table decoration are planned through the reservation.
  • Look at the menu. Current dishes and prices are on the digital menu.
  • For frequently asked questions see our FAQ page.

If you are wondering about a stop on the way or what to do in Kırkpınar before dinner, you can read our guide to where to eat in Kırkpınar.

On Arrival: Entrance, Valet and Getting to Your Table

When you arrive at the entrance of Didi Otel, the valet takes your car; if you would rather park yourself, the lot is free too. You can recognize the restaurant's entrance by the navy blue MARE awning. If you have a reservation, just give your name; the team will direct you to your table in the garden or in the indoor dining room.

Tips for Weekend Traffic

  • Friday evening is the busiest time leaving Istanbul; if you can, set off before 17:00 or after 20:00.
  • On Sunday evening traffic back towards Istanbul builds up; moving dinner to a later hour can make the return easier.
  • If you will be late, let us know on WhatsApp; your table will be held.
