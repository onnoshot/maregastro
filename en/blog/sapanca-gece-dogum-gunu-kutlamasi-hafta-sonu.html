Not everyone's schedule fits the daytime. For those who want to celebrate a birthday late at night in Sapanca, Mare Gastro being open 24 hours on weekends is a major advantage.

Is It Possible to Celebrate a Birthday at Night in Sapanca?

Yes. For those wanting to celebrate a night birthday in Sapanca, Mare Gastro is open throughout the weekend; a late celebration after work, or a table that stretches into midnight, is possible.

Friday, Saturday, Sunday: The 24-Hour Privilege

Mare Gastro is open 24 hours on Friday, Saturday and Sunday; closed Monday through Thursday. This flexibility gives guests planning a birthday in Sapanca the freedom to celebrate without watching the clock.
