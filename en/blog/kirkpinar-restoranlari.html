Where to Eat in Kırkpınar? A Sapanca Restaurant Guide

November 16, 20267 min readMare Gastro

Short answer: Kırkpınar, on the south side of Lake Sapanca, is the part of the district with the densest cluster of restaurants and hotels. Breakfast-focused cafe-restaurants line the Melih Kibar coast road, and hotel restaurants sit on the Tepebaşı side. For dinner and fine dining, Mare Gastro in the garden of Didi Otel in Kırkpınar Soğuksu is open until 00:00 Tuesday to Sunday.

Where Is Kırkpınar?

Kırkpınar is the part of Sapanca district that rests against the south side of the lake; it covers the Kırkpınar Soğuksu and Kırkpınar Tepebaşı neighborhoods. The Melih Kibar coast road, running along the water, is the axis where the area's cafes and restaurants gather. The accommodation side is covered by our sister business in Didi Otel's Kırkpınar accommodation guide (in Turkish); this post focuses on dining options.

Breakfast or Dinner in Kırkpınar?

Kırkpınar's coast road is best known for weekend breakfast; in the morning hours it gets crowded. In the evening the options narrow: some breakfast-focused businesses close early, and dinner is eaten mostly at hotel restaurants and at places open until late.

Parking and Weekend Crowds in Kırkpınar

Kırkpınar's coast road gets busy at weekends, especially at breakfast time; finding a parking space in front of the businesses on the coast road can become difficult. That is why choosing venues with their own parking lot, or arriving early, saves time. At Mare Gastro parking is free and there is valet at the entrance; you can leave your car and spend the day on foot.

Is There Somewhere to Drink Alcohol in Kırkpınar?

Yes; some restaurants and hotel restaurants in the area serve alcohol. Mare Gastro has 31 rakı, 32 wine, 4 champagne and sparkling and 12 alcoholic cocktail options (November 2026). At other venues we recommend asking about alcohol service beforehand. The detailed list is in our post on alcohol-serving restaurants.

How Do You Get to Mare Gastro from Kırkpınar?

Mare Gastro is in Kırkpınar Soğuksu on İstasyon 3 Street, in the garden of Didi Otel and right next to Lake Sapanca. It shares the hotel's entrance and parking lot; parking is free and valet service is available.

For a one-tap route on the map, see our post on how to get to Mare Gastro.

What to Eat at Mare Gastro for Dinner in Kırkpınar

The backbone of the menu is Aegean-Mediterranean: starters such as Crete-Style Cheese Spread and hummus, warm small plates such as Mare Special Grilled Baby Calamari and Mare Special Octopus Casserole, and main courses such as Mare Special Meagre Fish (rock sea bass), Beef Tenderloin in Pepper Cream Sauce and Lamb Chops. You can get to know Executive Chef Doğan Anapa's kitchen in our chef post; you can book your table through the reservation form.

5 Tips for Eating in Kırkpınar

  1. Head out early for breakfast. At weekends the coast road is busiest in the late morning.
  2. Book for the evening. Because few places stay open in the evening, weekend tables fill quickly.
  3. Check Monday. Some businesses are closed on Mondays; Mare Gastro is closed on Mondays too.
  4. Ask about alcohol service. Not every venue serves alcohol.
  5. Bring a cardigan. Evenings around the lake can cool down even in summer.

Kırkpınar at Night: Live Music and Late Hours

Kırkpınar's nightlife is quiet; people who come to the area at night mostly look for a long dinner at a restaurant that is open late. Mare Gastro is open until 00:00 Tuesday to Sunday, and most weekend evenings there is live music. You can follow the program in our live music post and read about late options in our late-night restaurant post.

Breakfast in Kırkpınar: Mare Gastro's Turkish Breakfast Spread

For those who come to Kırkpınar for breakfast, Mare Gastro opens at 09:00; for guests staying at Didi Otel, breakfast is served here as a plated breakfast, and the serpme breakfast can be chosen for an extra charge. Guests with children are welcome until 15:00. According to the menu, the serpme breakfast includes black and grilled green olives, apple-cinnamon and strawberry jam, acuka, flower honey, clotted cream, butter, Ezine cheese, fresh kaşar, çeçil cheese, a plate of fresh vegetables, gözleme, pişi, sucuk fried in the pan, omelet or menemen, muska börek, melted cheese in a clay pot, seasonal fruit and tea. For those who want something lighter there is Brioche Benedict (poached egg, smoked meat, sautéed asparagus, hollandaise). Details are in our breakfast post.

How Do You Get to Kırkpınar?

Kırkpınar is west of Sapanca's town center, on the south side of the lake; it is reached by a short drive from the TEM and D-100 connections. For general access to Sapanca, see Didi Otel's travel guide (in Turkish), and for the route directly to Mare Gastro, our directions page.
