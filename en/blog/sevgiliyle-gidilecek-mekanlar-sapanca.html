Places to Go as a Couple: A Sapanca Guide for Two

2026-10-246 min readMare Gastro

A list of places to go as a couple is really the plan for a day: a little nature during the day, a view at sunset and a good table in the evening. Sapanca brings all three together about 1.5 hours from Istanbul. Below you'll find a one-day route for couples and our suggestion for the evening.

Where Are the Best Places to Go as a Couple?

Among the places to go as a couple around Istanbul, Sapanca is one of the easiest getaways to reach, with its lakeside, forest routes and gourmet restaurants. For dinner, Mare Gastro in the garden of Didi Otel is one of the addresses couples choose most, rated 4.9 on Google Maps and with its fine dining kitchen.

Why Mare Gastro for the Evening?

When a couple chooses a place for dinner, they usually look at three things: atmosphere, kitchen and trust. Mare Gastro is strong on all three:

  • Atmosphere: In the garden of Didi Otel, among pines and palms, right next to the lake. There is an open-air garden and an indoor dining room.
  • Kitchen: Sapanca's only fine dining restaurant, with Aegean-Mediterranean plates signed by Executive Chef Doğan Anapa.
  • Trust: Rated 4.9 on Google Maps; guests praise the atmosphere, the service team and special-occasion arrangements most.

Anniversaries and special evenings are what couples mention most in the reviews. For example:

Sapanca as a Couple, Season by Season

Sapanca shows couples a different face in every season: the freshest green and cool evenings in spring; the lakeside and long open-air evenings in summer; forest colours and a quieter town in autumn; and in winter, snow on Kartepe and a warm table.

Because Mare Gastro has both an open-air garden and an indoor dining room, your evening plan works whatever the season. We describe what a winter evening is like in our article on date nights in every season.

What Is Fine Dining, and Why Is It Different for Couples?

Fine dining is a high-end dining experience that brings together carefully chosen ingredients, finely crafted presentation, impeccable service and a thoughtfully designed atmosphere. For couples the difference is this: dinner isn't a meal you rush through but a conversation that lasts as the plates arrive one after another. For more on the concept, read What is fine dining?.

Celebrating a Special Day Together?

For a special day such as an anniversary, a birthday or a proposal, just leave the team a note when you book; the table setting, a cake or the surprise moment is planned in advance. Our guides by occasion: anniversary dinner, marriage proposal and a birthday dinner for your partner.

What to Look For When Choosing a Place as a Couple

  • Sound and privacy: Ask for a table where you can talk just the two of you.
  • Weather: A place with both indoor and outdoor seating removes the risk of your plan falling through.
  • Getting there: After a long drive, choose a place without parking trouble.
  • Staying over: If you're out of town, being able to stay at the same place makes the evening easier.
  • Reviews: Look at recent guest reviews more than photos.

Frequently Asked Questions

Mare Gastro · Sapanca
Where are good places to go as a couple?
For getaways near Istanbul, Sapanca stands out: a walk along the lake, nature towards Maşukiye and Kartepe, sunset and dinner. For the evening, Mare Gastro in the garden of Didi Otel is an address chosen by couples looking for fine dining.
Where can couples have dinner in Sapanca?
Mare Gastro, in the garden of Didi Otel right next to the lake, is one of the standout restaurants for a couple's dinner in Sapanca, with a 4.9 rating on Google Maps, an open-air garden and an indoor dining room.
Can couples visit Sapanca as a day trip?
Yes. Sapanca is about 1.5 hours from Istanbul, so a day trip works. If you'd rather not drive back, you can stay at Didi Otel, where Mare Gastro is located.
Does Mare Gastro arrange special occasions?
Yes. For anniversaries, birthdays and proposals, just leave the team a note when you book; the table and the surprise moment are planned in advance.
Who is Mare Gastro's chef?
Mare Gastro's Executive Chef is Doğan Anapa. Coming from a background in film, the chef interprets Aegean-Mediterranean cuisine with local produce such as Karasu asparagus and shares his recipes on YouTube.
