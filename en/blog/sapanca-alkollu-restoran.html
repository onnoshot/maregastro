A Restaurant Serving Alcohol in Sapanca: Rakı, Wine and Cocktails

Updated 2026-11-126 min readMare Gastro

Short answer: for a dinner with drinks in Sapanca, Mare Gastro, in the garden of Didi Otel right next to the lake, serves rakı, wine and cocktails alongside its fine dining kitchen. As of November 2026 the menu lists 31 rakı options, 32 wines, 4 champagnes and sparkling wines and 12 cocktails; weekend evenings come with live music.

Is There a Restaurant in Sapanca That Serves Alcohol?

Yes. There are restaurants in Sapanca that serve alcohol; Mare Gastro in Kırkpınar is one of them and serves drinks as part of a dining experience. The restaurant is open Tuesday to Sunday, 09:00–00:00, and closed on Mondays.

The menu ranges from a rakı and fish table to dinner with wine or a cocktail at sunset. For the current list and prices see our digital menu; the menu is updated with the season.

Is It Good for Rakı and Fish?

Yes. Mare Gastro's kitchen is seafood-focused and its starters are very close to the meze of a rakı table: hummus, beetroot borani, carrot tarator, Cretan cheese spread, a cheese plate, the artichoke in olive oil of the day and bonito carpaccio among them. Hot appetisers include fried calamari and the Mare Special Islama Calamari.

For meze choices and how to set the table, see our Sapanca rakı and fish guide.

What Are the Signature Cocktails?

Alongside the classics, the cocktail menu has recipes with a local touch. Kuzu Kulağı Sour is made with sorrel, and R.R. with rakı, rocket and lemon. Espresso Martini, Whisky Sour, Mojito and Margarita are also on the menu, as well as a non-alcoholic cocktail, cold drinks and hot drinks.

Is There Live Music?

Weekend evenings come with live music, and guest artists take the stage on special nights. We have collected the summer 2026 programme and current events in our Sapanca live music guide.

Can You Drink Outdoors?

Yes. Mare Gastro has an open-air garden and an indoor dining room. In summer you are welcomed in the garden, and on cool and rainy days indoors, with the same menu.

Can You Stay Over After Drinking?

Mare Gastro is in the garden of Didi Otel. Instead of driving after dinner you can stay at the hotel in the same garden; just mention it in the booking form. Parking is free and valet service is available.
