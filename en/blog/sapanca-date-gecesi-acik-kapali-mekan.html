Date Night in Every Season: A Romantic Restaurant in Sapanca With Indoor and Outdoor Seating

Updated 2026-11-126 min readMare Gastro

The weather is the most common reason date plans fall apart. If you don't want to dream of an evening outdoors and get caught in the rain, or be stuck in crowded indoor rooms in winter, choose a place with both outdoor and indoor seating. In Sapanca, Mare Gastro serves the same fine dining kitchen in two different settings.

Is There a Romantic Restaurant in Sapanca With Indoor and Outdoor Seating?

Yes. Mare Gastro, in the garden of Didi Otel, has both an open-air garden under the pine trees and an indoor dining room. In summer you dine outdoors in the cool air from the lake; on cool, rainy or winter evenings you are welcomed indoors by the same kitchen and the same service team. Being the only restaurant in Sapanca with a fine dining concept is another plus for a date.

5 Questions to Ask When Choosing a Place With Indoor and Outdoor Seating

  1. Is the same menu served indoors? In some places the indoor area is just a back-up room. At Mare Gastro the same kitchen serves both areas.
  2. Can the table be moved if the weather turns? Mention your preference when you book and the team will adjust your table.
  3. Is the indoor area good for conversation? On live-music evenings, ask for a table away from the stage.
  4. Can the outdoor area be used on cool evenings? On spring and autumn evenings we suggest bringing a cardigan.
  5. What to do after dinner? The option of staying at Didi Otel in the same garden makes the evening easier.

A Sample Date Programme for a Winter Evening

In the winter months a date day in Sapanca might look like this: leave Istanbul around midday, enjoy a snowy view towards Kartepe in the afternoon, take a short walk by the lake in the late afternoon and have the chef's warm signature plates in Mare Gastro's indoor dining room in the evening. You can spend the night at Didi Otel and head back after a lake-view breakfast the next morning.

Why Mare Gastro? The Short Answer

  • Two settings, one kitchen: Open-air garden and indoor dining room.
  • Fine dining: Sapanca's only fine dining restaurant, signed by Executive Chef Doğan Anapa.
  • Trust: Rated 4.9 on Google Maps (October 2026).
  • Location: Kırkpınar Soğuksu, İstasyon 3 Cad. No:55, Sapanca/Sakarya (garden of Didi Otel); about 1.5 hours from Istanbul.
  • Stay: You can stay at Didi Otel in the same garden.

How to Choose Indoor or Outdoor When You Book

Choose the date, time and party size in the booking form and send it on WhatsApp; then simply write “garden” or “indoor dining room” in the same WhatsApp conversation. If the weather changes, our team lets you know and adjusts your table. For a quick question you can write to our WhatsApp line.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which romantic restaurant in Sapanca has indoor seating?
Mare Gastro, in the garden of Didi Otel, has both an open-air garden and an indoor dining room. On cool, rainy and winter evenings guests are served indoors with the same fine dining kitchen.
Where to go for a date in Sapanca in winter?

Can you dine outdoors at Mare Gastro?
