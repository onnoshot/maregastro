Frequently Asked Questions

Official, up-to-date answers to the questions we hear most about Mare Gastro.

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Where is Mare Gastro?

Mare Gastro is in the garden of Didi Otel in Sapanca, right by Lake Sapanca. Address: Kırkpınar Soğuksu, İstasyon 3 Cad. No:55, 54600 Sapanca/Sakarya, Türkiye. Open in Google Maps.

What are your opening hours?

We are open Tuesday to Sunday, 09:00–00:00. We are closed on Mondays.

How do I make a reservation?

Use the three-step reservation form to choose your date, time and party size, then confirm via WhatsApp. Phone and WhatsApp: +90 532 354 08 88.

What kind of cuisine do you serve?

Seafood-focused Aegean and Mediterranean cuisine by Executive Chef Doğan Anapa. The menu has starters, salads, hot appetisers, main courses, breakfast plates, desserts and snacks.

Where can I see the menu and prices?

The current menu and prices are in our digital menu and in the menu section of our website.

Do you have a kids' menu?

Yes. Our menu has a separate Kids' Menu section.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes. Meat-free dishes include hummus, artichoke in olive oil, fried aubergine and peppers in tomato sauce and seasonal salads. Let us know about vegan or gluten-free needs when you book and the kitchen will prepare accordingly.

Do you serve alcohol?

Yes. Wines, champagnes, rakı, cocktails (with and without alcohol), beers and hot and cold drinks are on the menu.

Is there parking?

Yes. Parking is free and valet service is available.

Can I stay overnight?

Mare Gastro is part of Didi Otel. You can mention that you would like to stay when you book and spend a night by the lake at Didi Otel.

Do you have outdoor seating?

Yes. We have both indoor and outdoor seating, including tables in the garden.

Is there live music?

Live music accompanies weekend evenings. Special events are shown in the calendar of the reservation section.

Can I celebrate a birthday, anniversary or proposal?

Yes. Add a note when you book and our team will plan it with you. Ideas: birthdays, anniversaries, proposals.

How far is it from Istanbul?

Sapanca is about 1.5 hours from Istanbul by car. See our directions guide.

Who is your chef?

Our Executive Chef is Doğan Anapa, Gourmand 2023 Türkiye representative, who runs a seasonal kitchen with a storyteller's eye shaped by his background in film. Who is Chef Doğan Anapa?

What do guests say?

We are rated 4.9 on Google Maps (October 2026).
