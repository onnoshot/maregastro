  1. Date

    Pick the day you would like to come. Closed days are shown in the calendar.

    Choose a time (09:00–00:00, every half hour), party size, your name, phone and whether you would like to stay overnight.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a table at Mare Gastro?

The quickest way is the booking form on our website: choose the date, time and party size, send it on WhatsApp and our team confirms there. You can also message us on WhatsApp directly or call +90 532 354 08 88.

Which days and times can I book?

We take bookings Tuesday to Sunday between 09:00 and 00:00, every half hour. We are closed on Mondays. Breakfast, lunch and dinner all use the same form.

What about large groups or private events?

Selecting 10 or more guests in the form marks your request as a private organisation, and we plan the details with you on WhatsApp. Ideas: group dinners and corporate dinners.

Can you help with a birthday, anniversary or proposal?

Yes. Tell us about the occasion when you book or on WhatsApp and we will plan the table setting, cake and menu with you. Ideas: birthdays, anniversaries, proposals, engagement dinners.

Can I arrange an overnight stay with the same booking?

Yes. Choose "I would like to stay" in the form and your request for a room at Didi Otel, right by the lake, is passed on too. Didi Otel

When is my booking confirmed?

Once the form is sent on WhatsApp, our team replies there as soon as possible and confirms your table.

How do I get there, and is there parking?

Mare Gastro is in the garden of Didi Otel in Sapanca, right next to Lake Sapanca, about 1.5 hours from Istanbul. Parking is free and valet service is available. How to get to Sapanca

Can I see the menu before booking?

Yes. The current menu and prices are in our digital menu. For other questions see our FAQ.
